AI reporting for Magento: ask your store data in plain English

By Gaurav Harsh, Magento developer · 21 November 2026 · Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4

Short answer: AI reporting lets anyone on your team type a question such as "How many orders did the UK website get last month, by status?" in the Magento admin, and get the answer or a table they can download, without building a report or waiting for a developer.

How it works

  1. You ask in everyday language, and choose a short answer or a table.
  2. The AI prepares a query. It reads a snapshot of how your store is set up (store views, attributes, order statuses, the tables your extensions added) and checks details such as SKUs before it writes the query.
  3. The query runs read-only on your own database, through a separate user that can't change anything.
  4. You get the result: a written answer with the assumptions it made, or a table with a CSV download. The exact query is one click away.

What you can ask

Anything the Magento database knows: orders and revenue, customers and customer groups, products and attributes, stock, promotions, store setup, and "why" questions such as "Why is this product not showing in the French store view?". See 50 example questions.

Why a general chatbot isn't enough

Magento's database is unusual: product details are spread across many tables, values can differ per store view, Adobe Commerce adds versioning, and every store has its own extensions. A general AI doesn't know any of that about your store, so its queries fail or quietly return the wrong number. Seven mistakes ChatGPT makes with Magento SQL.

How accurate is it?

On an Adobe Commerce store with about 700 modules, AI Reports answered 9 of 9 real support questions correctly. That's a small test, so every answer shows what it assumed and the query it ran, and your team can mark answers right or wrong. Answers marked right are reused for similar questions.

What does it cost to run?

You use your own AI provider account (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible service), so you pay them per question. In that same test, a question cost about $0.05 with Claude Sonnet 5.5 and about $0.11 with Claude Opus 5.5. Saved reports re-run at no AI cost, and you can set a monthly budget and a daily limit per person.

What it can't do

  • It only knows your Magento database: no Google Analytics, ads, ERP or email platform data.
  • It can be wrong. Read the "Assumed" line and check any figure you plan to share.
  • Every question stands alone: it doesn't remember the one before.

Is it safe? Read the 10-point safety checklist, and how AI Reports answers each point.

The quick way

Ask AI Reports in plain English, from the Magento admin:

“How many orders did the UK website get last month, by status?”

You get the numbers first, then the evidence and what it assumed. It runs read-only on your own store, in Answer mode, in under a minute. No SQL needed.
