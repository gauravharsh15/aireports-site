50 questions you can ask your Magento store data

By Gaurav Harsh, Magento developer · 21 November 2026 · Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4

Short answer: if it's in the Magento database, you can ask it. Below are 50 questions store teams ask most, grouped by topic. Use them with an AI reporting tool, or hand them to your developer.

Tip: a good question has four parts: what (number of orders, top 10 SKUs), where (website, store view, customer group), when (last month, 1–15 November) and how (by status, with SKU and price). "Table" marks questions whose answer is a list you'll want as a spreadsheet.

  • What was our revenue per website last month, excluding canceled orders?Answer

  • Which payment methods were used last month, and how many orders each?Answer
  • Which shipping methods do customers choose most?Answer
  • Which orders are still pending after 3 days?Table
  • Which countries did our orders come from last quarter?Answer
  • How many orders used a coupon code last month, and which codes?Answer
  • Compare orders this October with October last year, per website.Answer
  • What share of orders came from guests and what share from registered customers?Answer

Replace the SKU, store view, website and dates with your own. These questions only read data from Magento; they don't cover Google Analytics, ads or other systems.
