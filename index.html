06 / Safe by design

Read-only, at every layer.

It runs AI-written SQL against production, so safety never rests on a single check.

Anyone with access reads every non-blocked table across all websites, so grant it only to roles that may see that data.

  • A separate read-only MySQL user, set only in env.php, plus a forced read-only session.
  • One SELECT per query. Comments, multiple statements, variables, system schemas and file functions are rejected.
  • Credential tables are never readable, such as admin users, tokens, API keys, payment tokens and config. The AI is never told they exist.
  • Row limits and a statement timeout on every query.
  • Personal and payment data is masked in anything sent back to the AI. Table + CSV mode sends no rows at all.
  • Full audit log of every question, query, user, token count and cost.
  • Your AI key, your data. Questions go straight from your server to your AI provider. We never see them.

09 / Questions

Good questions, answered.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. Ask for a free 14-day trial key and install it on your store, or try the free open-source version 2 on GitHub. Every plan also comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee.

What if it doesn't work for our store?

E-mail us within 14 days of buying and you get a full refund, no questions asked. We'd still like to hear what went wrong, because that's how the product gets better.

Does my store data go to you?

No. Questions, queries and results go from your server to the AI provider you choose, under your own account. The only call to us is a daily licence check that sends the licence key, your domain and the module and Magento versions.

What does the AI usage cost?

On our test store a question averaged $0.11 with Claude Opus 5.5, from $0.04 for simple counts to $0.37 for a full "why isn't this showing" diagnosis. The Quick preset costs about half. Saved reports and re-used approved answers cost nothing.

Which AI providers work?

Anthropic (recommended), OpenAI, Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. You pick a model for each quality preset.

Can it change or delete data?

No. It connects as a separate MySQL user that only has SELECT, forces a read-only session, and rejects anything that isn't a single SELECT before it reaches the database.

Can I use one licence on staging and production?

Yes. One key works on every environment. Local, test and staging domains, including Adobe Commerce Cloud integration and staging environments, never count towards your limit; only production stores do.

What happens if I don't renew?

Everything keeps working. You keep every version released while your plan was active, and Composer can still install them. You stop getting new versions, rule packs and support until you renew.

What are the requirements?

Magento Open Source or Adobe Commerce 2.4 on PHP 8.2 or 8.3, MySQL or MariaDB, and an API key from an AI provider. Tested on Adobe Commerce 2.4.8 with MariaDB 10.6.

Give your team the answers.

Install it in about 15 minutes, keep your data on your server, and stop writing tickets for simple questions.
