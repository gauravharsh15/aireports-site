Magento 2 catalog_product_entity_decimal table

By Gaurav Harsh, Magento developer · 21 November 2026 · Checked against Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.8

Decimal product values: price, special_price, cost, weight and other decimal attributes.

Key columns

ColumnWhat it means
attribute_idWhich attribute, linking to eav_attribute.
store_id0 = default. Prices are usually stored only at 0 unless your catalog price scope is set to Website.
entity_id (Open Source) / row_id (Adobe Commerce)The product, or on Adobe Commerce the product version.
valueThe number, with up to 6 decimals.

Good to know

  • The price a shopper actually pays also depends on special prices, tier prices, catalog price rules and customer group. The storefront reads the result from catalog_product_index_price.

Example: Products with a price of 0

Magento Open Source

SELECT e.sku, e.type_id, p.value AS price
FROM catalog_product_entity e
JOIN eav_attribute a ON a.attribute_code = 'price'
JOIN eav_entity_type t ON t.entity_type_id = a.entity_type_id AND t.entity_type_code = 'catalog_product'
JOIN catalog_product_entity_decimal p ON p.entity_id = e.entity_id AND p.attribute_id = a.attribute_id AND p.store_id = 0
WHERE p.value = 0
ORDER BY e.sku;

Adobe Commerce

SELECT e.sku, e.type_id, p.value AS price
FROM catalog_product_entity e
JOIN eav_attribute a ON a.attribute_code = 'price'
JOIN eav_entity_type t ON t.entity_type_id = a.entity_type_id AND t.entity_type_code = 'catalog_product'
JOIN catalog_product_entity_decimal p ON p.row_id = e.row_id AND p.attribute_id = a.attribute_id AND p.store_id = 0
WHERE p.value = 0
  AND e.created_in <= UNIX_TIMESTAMP() AND e.updated_in > UNIX_TIMESTAMP()
ORDER BY e.sku;

Tested on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.8. Read-only. If your tables have a prefix, add it to each table name.

Extra columns on Adobe Commerce

Adobe Commerce has 5 columns here instead of 5. The extra ones come from these Commerce features:

  • Catalog Staging: row_id

On Adobe Commerce, entity_id is replaced by row_id, which points at a product version (catalog_product_entity.row_id).

Foreign keys

  • attribute_id → eav_attribute.attribute_id
  • entity_id → catalog_product_entity.entity_id
  • store_id → store.store_id
  • row_id → catalog_product_entity.row_id Adobe Commerce
