Magento 2 customer_address_entity table

By Gaurav Harsh, Magento developer · 21 November 2026 · Checked against Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.8

Customers' saved address book entries. A customer can have many; their defaults are chosen on customer_entity.

Key columns

ColumnWhat it means
entity_idAddress ID.
parent_idThe customer, linking to customer_entity.entity_id.
firstname / lastname / companyThe name on the address.
streetStreet lines, separated by a new line.
city / region / region_id / postcodeTown, state or county, and postcode.
country_idTwo-letter country code.
telephone / vat_idPhone number and VAT number.

Good to know

  • Changing an address here doesn't change past orders: they keep their own copy in sales_order_address.

Example: Customers per country (default billing address)

Works on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce.

SELECT a.country_id, COUNT(*) AS customers
FROM customer_entity c
JOIN customer_address_entity a ON a.entity_id = c.default_billing
GROUP BY a.country_id
ORDER BY customers DESC;

Tested on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.8. Read-only. If your tables have a prefix, add it to each table name.
