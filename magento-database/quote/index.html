Guides › Database reference › Carts

Magento 2 quote table

By Gaurav Harsh, Magento developer · 21 November 2026 · Checked against Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.8

Shopping carts. Every cart, from guests and logged-in customers, is a row here, whether or not it became an order.

Key columns

ColumnWhat it means
entity_idInternal cart ID. Orders keep it in sales_order.quote_id.
store_idThe store view of the cart.
customer_id / customer_emailThe customer, or NULL for guests until they enter an email.
is_active1 = still a cart. 0 = turned into an order or replaced.
items_count / items_qtyNumber of product lines and total quantity.
base_grand_totalCart total in your base currency.
reserved_order_idThe order number reserved for it at checkout.
created_at / updated_atWhen the cart was created and last changed.

Good to know

  • Old carts are deleted by cron after the Quote Lifetime (days) setting (Stores › Configuration › Sales › Checkout › Shopping Cart, 30 days by default).
  • Abandoned carts are usually active carts with items that haven't been updated for a while.

Example: Active carts with items in the last 7 days, and their value

Works on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce.

SELECT COUNT(*) AS carts, ROUND(SUM(base_grand_total), 2) AS value
FROM quote
WHERE is_active = 1
  AND items_count > 0
  AND updated_at >= NOW() - INTERVAL 7 DAY;

Tested on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.8. Read-only. If your tables have a prefix, add it to each table name.

Extra columns on Adobe Commerce

Adobe Commerce has 84 columns here instead of 51. The extra ones come from these Commerce features:

  • Customer Balance: customer_balance_amount_used, base_customer_bal_amount_used, use_customer_balance
  • Gift Card Account: gift_cards, gift_cards_amount, base_gift_cards_amount, gift_cards_amount_used, base_gift_cards_amount_used
  • Gift Wrapping: gw_id, gw_allow_gift_receipt, gw_add_card, gw_base_price, gw_price, gw_items_base_price, gw_items_price, gw_card_base_price, gw_card_price, gw_base_tax_amount, gw_tax_amount, gw_items_base_tax_amount, gw_items_tax_amount, gw_card_base_tax_amount, gw_card_tax_amount, gw_base_price_incl_tax, gw_price_incl_tax, gw_items_base_price_incl_tax, gw_items_price_incl_tax, gw_card_base_price_incl_tax, gw_card_price_incl_tax
  • Reward: use_reward_points, reward_points_balance, base_reward_currency_amount, reward_currency_amount
