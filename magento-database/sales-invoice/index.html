Magento 2 sales_invoice table

By Gaurav Harsh, Magento developer · 21 November 2026 · Checked against Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.8

One row per invoice. An order can have several invoices if it was invoiced in parts.

Key columns

ColumnWhat it means
entity_idInternal invoice ID.
order_idThe order, linking to sales_order.entity_id.
increment_idThe invoice number shown in the admin and in emails.
state1 = open (pending), 2 = paid, 3 = canceled.
grand_total / base_grand_totalInvoice total in the order currency / your base currency.
subtotal / tax_amount / shipping_amountThe parts of the total.
created_atWhen the invoice was created, in UTC.

Good to know

  • Invoice lines are in sales_invoice_item; credit memos (refunds) are in sales_creditmemo.
  • Revenue by invoice date and revenue by order date are different numbers. Decide which one your report needs.

Example: Invoiced revenue per month this year

Works on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce.

SELECT DATE_FORMAT(created_at, '%Y-%m') AS month, COUNT(*) AS invoices, ROUND(SUM(base_grand_total), 2) AS invoiced
FROM sales_invoice
WHERE created_at >= MAKEDATE(YEAR(NOW()), 1)
  AND state = 2                     -- paid
GROUP BY month
ORDER BY month;

Tested on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.8. Read-only. If your tables have a prefix, add it to each table name.

Extra columns on Adobe Commerce

Adobe Commerce has 66 columns here instead of 47. The extra ones come from these Commerce features:

  • Customer Balance: base_customer_balance_amount, customer_balance_amount
  • Gift Card Account: base_gift_cards_amount, gift_cards_amount
  • Gift Wrapping: gw_base_price, gw_price, gw_items_base_price, gw_items_price, gw_card_base_price, gw_card_price, gw_base_tax_amount, gw_tax_amount, gw_items_base_tax_amount, gw_items_tax_amount, gw_card_base_tax_amount, gw_card_tax_amount
  • Reward: base_reward_currency_amount, reward_currency_amount, reward_points_balance

Foreign keys

  • order_id → sales_order.entity_id
  • store_id → store.store_id
