Magento 2 sales_order_item table

By Gaurav Harsh, Magento developer · 21 November 2026 · Checked against Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.8

One row per product line in an order. Configurable and bundle products have a parent row plus child rows, so the same sale can appear twice.

Key columns

ColumnWhat it means
item_idInternal ID of the order line.
order_idThe order, linking to sales_order.entity_id.
parent_item_idNULL for a top-level line. For the chosen variant of a configurable product (or a bundle part), the parent line's item_id.
product_idThe product's ID at the time of ordering. On the parent line of a configurable product, it's the configurable product.
sku / nameCopied at order time. On the parent line of a configurable product, sku is the chosen variant's SKU.
product_typesimple, configurable, bundle, virtual and so on.
qty_orderedQuantity ordered. Also qty_invoiced, qty_shipped, qty_refunded and qty_canceled.
price / row_totalUnit price and price × quantity, before discount and without tax. base_ versions are in your base currency.
discount_amountThe line's discount, stored as a positive number (unlike sales_order).

Good to know

  • To count a sale once, use only top-level lines: parent_item_id IS NULL.
  • For configurable products, the child (variant) line has a price of 0. The price is on the parent line.
  • Order lines keep the SKU and name from the time of the order, even if the product is renamed or deleted later.

Example: Top 10 products by quantity sold, last 30 days

Works on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce.

SELECT i.sku, MAX(i.name) AS name, SUM(i.qty_ordered) AS qty, ROUND(SUM(i.base_row_total), 2) AS revenue
FROM sales_order_item i
JOIN sales_order o ON o.entity_id = i.order_id
WHERE o.created_at >= NOW() - INTERVAL 30 DAY
  AND o.state NOT IN ('canceled', 'closed')
  AND i.parent_item_id IS NULL      -- one row per product line
GROUP BY i.sku
ORDER BY qty DESC
LIMIT 10;

Tested on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.8. Read-only. If your tables have a prefix, add it to each table name.

Extra columns on Adobe Commerce

Adobe Commerce has 96 columns here instead of 80. The extra ones come from these Commerce features:

  • Catalog Event: event_id
  • Gift Registry: giftregistry_item_id
  • Gift Wrapping: gw_id, gw_base_price, gw_price, gw_base_tax_amount, gw_tax_amount, gw_base_price_invoiced, gw_price_invoiced, gw_base_tax_amount_invoiced, gw_tax_amount_invoiced, gw_base_price_refunded, gw_price_refunded, gw_base_tax_amount_refunded, gw_tax_amount_refunded
  • Rma: qty_returned

Foreign keys

  • order_id → sales_order.entity_id
  • store_id → store.store_id
