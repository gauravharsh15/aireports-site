How do I find abandoned carts in Magento?

By Gaurav Harsh, Magento developer · 26 November 2026 · Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4

Short answer: Carts are stored in quote. An active quote with items that hasn't become an order is an abandoned cart. Magento's report shows registered customers only; the query includes guests who entered an email.

Check it step by step

These queries only read data. Run them on a copy of your database if you can, or ask your developer to. If your tables have a prefix (set in app/etc/env.php), add it to each table name.

Carts with items, idle for over an hour, from the last 7 days

Works on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce:

SELECT q.entity_id AS quote_id, q.customer_email, q.customer_id IS NOT NULL AS registered,
       q.items_count, q.items_qty, ROUND(q.base_grand_total, 2) AS cart_value, q.updated_at
FROM quote q
WHERE q.is_active = 1
  AND q.items_count > 0
  AND q.reserved_order_id IS NULL
  AND q.updated_at BETWEEN NOW() - INTERVAL 7 DAY AND NOW() - INTERVAL 1 HOUR
ORDER BY cart_value DESC
LIMIT 100;

What's in them

Works on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce:

SELECT qi.sku, qi.name, COUNT(DISTINCT q.entity_id) AS carts, SUM(qi.qty) AS units
FROM quote q
JOIN quote_item qi ON qi.quote_id = q.entity_id AND qi.parent_item_id IS NULL
WHERE q.is_active = 1
  AND q.items_count > 0
  AND q.updated_at >= NOW() - INTERVAL 7 DAY
GROUP BY qi.sku, qi.name
ORDER BY carts DESC
LIMIT 25;

How to fix it

  • The built-in report is Reports › Marketing › Abandoned Carts (registered customers only).
  • Quotes are deleted after Stores › Configuration › Sales › Checkout › Shopping Cart › Quote Lifetime (days), 30 by default, by cron.
  • Emailing guests about their cart needs their consent under GDPR and similar laws; check before you build a reminder.
