Privacy

Short version: we don't receive your store's data. The module talks to the AI provider you choose, under your own account.

What we receive

WhenWhatWhy
You buy a licenceName, e-mail, billing country and payment details, collected by Polar, our reseller and merchant of record. We see the name, e-mail and order, not card details.To issue and renew your licence, and to contact you about it
Once a day, from your storeLicence key, the store's main domain, the module version and the Magento edition and versionTo confirm the licence and count production domains
Composer install or updateLicence key, IP address and the package version requestedTo serve the versions your licence covers
You visit this websitePage views, the page you came from, your country and browser, counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics without cookies and without following you to other sitesTo see how many people visit and which pages help them

What we never receive

Questions your team asks, generated SQL, query results, customer or order data, your AI provider key, or your database credentials.

Your AI provider

Questions, store structure and, in Answer mode, up to 20 masked result rows per query go from your server directly to the AI provider you configure. Their terms and data-retention policy apply. Ask them about zero data retention if customer data must not be retained.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep licence and domain records while your licence exists and for as long as tax law requires afterwards. Ask us to delete your records at any time, except what we must keep for tax.

Data is stored with Supabase in the region we chose for the project and is not sold or shared, except with Polar to process purchases.
