Releases

What changed in each version, newest first. To update, follow Docs › Updating. If you stop renewing, you keep every version released while your plan was active.

3.0.2 21 November 2026

PHP 8.4 support

  • Runs on PHP 8.4. Magento 2.4.8 supports PHP 8.4, and AI Reports now installs there too. PHP 8.2, 8.3 and 8.4 are supported. Tested on Adobe Commerce 2.4.8 with PHP 8.3 and 8.4.
  • Clearer documentation: re-using an approved answer makes no AI call in Table + CSV mode and one AI call in Answer mode; a question runs up to 2 queries by default.
  • The licence terms and the expiry notice no longer mention rule packs.
  • No database changes. After updating, the store snapshot rebuilds overnight, or run bin/magento aireports:knowledge:build straight away.

3.0.1 20 November 2026

More accurate answers on Adobe Commerce

  • Product links on Adobe Commerce. Configurable, bundle and grouped links, related products, custom options, tier prices, price rules and CMS store assignments point at a product version (row_id) on Adobe Commerce, not the ID the admin shows. AI Reports now reads which columns these are from your own database, third-party tables included, so "why isn't this product showing?" answers no longer claim that linked variants aren't linked.
  • Visibility "Catalog" and "Search" are told apart, and stock answers use the website's own stock and account for MSI reservations.
  • The Additional Instructions setting was removed. Put that text in business rules; the AI Knowledge page shows the old text, and how to delete it, until you do.
  • After updating, the store snapshot rebuilds overnight, or run bin/magento aireports:knowledge:build straight away.

3.0.0 17 November 2026

First commercial release

  • It learns your store. A snapshot of your installation (store views, every attribute and its options, every table and column and the module that owns it, and the real values of code columns), rebuilt weekly and after changes.
  • Business rules in plain words for what the database can't tell, plus the AI Knowledge page: test what the AI would be told, browse the snapshot, and review answers marked wrong.
  • Answer and Table + CSV modes, and Quick, Standard and Thorough per question. Thorough double-checks before you see the answer.
  • Checks before it guesses: it looks up the real store view, SKU, category or column values before writing a query, and tries again when nothing matched.
  • Learning with approval: 👍 turns an answer into an example, and the same question asked again re-runs the approved query.
  • Saved reports that anyone with access can re-run at no AI cost.
  • Usage and budget: cost per question, a monthly budget, and a daily limit per person.
  • Stronger privacy: personal and payment data masked before anything goes to the AI, also inside JSON and renamed columns, and raw payment tables always blocked.
  • Licence key, private Composer repository, and command-line tools to build and check the snapshot, copy business rules, and measure accuracy on your own questions.
